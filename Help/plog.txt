Log shows you the chat log of the channel you are currently in.
Plog shows you your pmsg log.
Log and Plog also has some cool sub commands as well.
You can grep for certian words or phrases.  
You can also specify how many entries you would like to see (default is 20). 
These commands may be combined as long as the number of entries is the last parameter.

/chat ServerName log [grep word or phrase][number to show]  IE:

/chat Servername log cpk     - This would return the last 20 chats in your current channel containing cpk in the chat
/chat Servername log cpk 100 - Same as above except would return the last 100 chats
/chat Servername log         - Displays last 20 chats
/chat Servername log 100     - Displays last 100 chats

Each channel can hold up to 5000 chats.
Plog is unlimited.
